Stage 01
Controlled Dewatering
Approx 10 - 15 Mins Step 1 of 7

Submersible Controlled Dewatering

The team deploys high-output, low-turbulence submersible pumps with automated float switches. We drain the top layer of standing water without churning up the settled mud at the bottom, directing water cleanly into stormwater channels to prevent rooftop puddling and terrace seepage.

Machinery: 1.5 HP Stainless Steel Impeller Submersible Pump (Discharge: 450 Liters/min).
Approx 10 - 15 Mins Step 2 of 7

Slurry & Heavy Sludge Extraction

Thick layers of decomposed organic matter, municipal silt, and sand settle into hard cakes at the tank bottom corners. We deploy heavy-duty slurry extractors that liquefy and siphon this muck directly into mobile containment receptacles without dirtying your building drainage pipes.

Machinery: Vortex Slurry Induction Pump with 2-inch non-clogging reinforced hose.
Stage 02
Slurry & Sludge Extraction
Stage 03
150-Bar Rotary Hydro-Jetting
Approx 15 - 20 Mins Step 3 of 7

150-Bar Rotary Hydro-Jetting

Calibrated German rotary pressure nozzles spray high-velocity water blades against the ceiling, vertical walls, steps, and bottom floor. The spinning hydro-blade shears away calcified mineral lines and biofilm sheets without needing abrasive wire scrubbers that scratch polymers.

Machinery: Kärcher Industrial Triplex Ceramic Plunger Pump (Operating Pressure: 150 Bar).
Approx 10 Mins Step 4 of 7

Industrial Vacuum Mopping

A high-vacuum wet extraction unit is wheeled in to mop up all dirty water droplets, suspended grit, and microscopic silt from tank floor recesses, leaving the interior dry and completely free of residual mud puddles before chemical sterilization.

Machinery: 3200W Dual-Bypass Industrial Wet-Dry Vacuum Extractor.
Stage 04
Industrial Vacuum Mopping
Stage 05
Bio-Organic Fogging
Approx 10 Mins Step 5 of 7

Food-Grade Bio-Organic Anti-Bacterial Fogging

We atomize a calibrated solution of bio-organic potassium permanganate and oxygenating compounds across all interior surfaces. This penetrates microscopic micro-fissures to destroy anaerobic bacterial colonies without creating chemical fumes or chlorine gas.

Compound: Pure food-grade NSF/ANSI Standard 60 bio-certified anti-microbial formulation.
Approx 15 Mins Step 6 of 7

Germicidal UV-C Radiator Sterilization

A high-intensity germicidal UV radiator is lowered into the empty tank. Operating at a peak wavelength of 253.7nm, ultraviolet photons shatter the DNA and RNA cellular bonds of residual mold spores, cysts, viruses, and Giardia lamblia.

Machinery: 254nm Quartz Sleeve UV-C Germicidal Radiator (Dosage: >30,000 µW·s/cm²).
Stage 06
254nm UV-C Radiation
Stage 07
Purity Audit & Warranty
Approx 5 Mins Step 7 of 7

Digital Purity Audit & Dashboard Certificate

The lead engineer performs on-site digital turbidity (NTU), TDS, and pH tests. High-resolution before and after photographs are uploaded to your customer portal, and a digital 30-day warranty certificate is generated.

Deliverable: Instant Digital Service Card, turbidity score, and next cycle reminder in your account.
