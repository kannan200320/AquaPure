Why Rooftop Tank Sludge Is Risky For Children

Children's skin is 30% thinner than adult skin and absorbs environmental toxins much faster during everyday bathing, brushing, and rinsing.

Infant Eczema & Skin Rashes

Stagnant tank biofilm harbors Pseudomonas and fungal colonies that trigger stubborn baby eczema, itchy scalp, and severe allergic flare-ups.

Aerosol Shower Inhalation

Hot water showers atomize tank microbes into fine airborne water droplets, causing Legionella and respiratory wheezing in kids and seniors.

Baby Bottle & Food Washing

Washing baby bottles, fruits, and vegetables in tap water connected to a sludge-laden tank introduces coliform bacteria directly into meals.

Health Best Practices

The 6 Golden Rules For Pure Household Water

01

Quarterly Tank Cleans

Never let more than 90 days elapse between cleanings, particularly during warmer summer or monsoon periods.

02

Inspect Tank Lid Seals

Ensure rooftop tank lids have airtight threaded seals to prevent airborne bird droppings and insect nests.

03

Clean Overflow Pipe Mesh

Fit stainless steel insect meshes on overflow pipes so mosquitoes cannot enter to lay eggs.

04

Flush Internal Pipe Runs

Perform high-pressure pipe de-scaling once a year to dislodge hardened calcification in wall lines.

05

Avoid Manual Bleaching

Bleaching powder erodes plastic tanks, corrodes brass plumbing, and leaves toxic chloramines.

06

Request Digital Lab Proof

Always verify post-cleaning water turbidity (under 1.0 NTU) and zero coliform count on your report.
