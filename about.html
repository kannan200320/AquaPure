Our Humble Origin

How An Unsettling Discovery Sparked A Regional Movement

In 2014, our founder, an environmental engineer, climbed up to inspect his own family's residential rooftop water tank after his young son developed recurrent gastroenteritis. What he discovered was alarming: six inches of putrid black mud, dead geckos, and thick sheets of toxic biofilm coating the tank walls.

When he hired traditional local cleaners, they scrubbed the tank with a dirty street broom and threw in cheap caustic bleaching powder that burned the eyes and corroded brass plumbing. He knew modern homeowners deserved scientific, hygienic, and mechanized water tank care. AquaPure was engineered from the ground up to solve this exact crisis.

Zero Compromise

Our Confined Space Safety Protocol

Underground sumps present hazardous environments with oxygen depletion and toxic biogas buildup (methane, hydrogen sulfide). AquaPure enforces strict life-safety standards:

Forced Air Ventilation

High-velocity centrifugal blowers continuously pump fresh ambient air into underground sumps.

Multi-Gas Electronic Sniffers

Technicians measure oxygen percentage (>19.5%) and verify zero toxic fumes before manhole entry.

Full PPE & Retrieval Harnesses

Trained standby supervisor stationed at the sump manhole at all times with certified winch harness.

Green Chemistry

Why We Refuse Chlorine Bleaching Powders

Traditional cleaners pour cheap commercial calcium hypochlorite into tanks. This creates corrosive hydrochloric acid, damages brass fittings, degrades plastic tanks, and creates carcinogenic trihalomethanes (THMs).

  • Potassium Permanganate micro-mist neutralizes organic odor without chemical fumes.
  • Food-grade bio-oxygen effervescence lifts stubborn limescale safely.
  • 100% bio-degradable wash water that will not harm municipal storm drains or soil.
