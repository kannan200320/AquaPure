Active Contamination Emergency? Act Immediately

Follow these immediate protective measures before our mobile van arrives.

Step 1: Shut Main Outlet Valve

Close the main discharge valve leading from the overhead tank to your household fixtures to prevent contaminated water from entering interior pipelines.

Step 2: Do Not Run Booster Pumps

Avoid powering on submersible or pressure pumps which force bottom sludge and sediment deposits into your kitchen RO filters and water heaters.

Step 3: Dial Our Hotline Immediately

Call 1-800-AQUA-PURE. Mention "Code Red Water Contamination" to bump your appointment to top priority dispatch queue.
